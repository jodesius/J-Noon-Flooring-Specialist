What we do

The flooring systems we install, and the order we work through each project.

Laminate

Hard-wearing, budget-friendly, and a huge range of looks.

  1. Check the subfloor and level any dips (ply or latex screed as needed)
  2. Lay the damp-proof membrane and underlay
  3. Set out the room and dry-lay so the boards run square
  4. Click the planks in with expansion gaps, then fit beading and trim the doors

LVT

Warm underfoot, waterproof, realistic wood and stone finishes.

  1. Skim the subfloor with smoothing compound for a flat, smooth base
  2. Prime, and overlay timber floors with flooring-grade ply
  3. Set out from the centre so the edge cuts are balanced
  4. Glue down to the pattern, roll the floor, add any borders or feature strips

Amtico

Premium LVT with bespoke design and border options.

  1. Moisture-test the subfloor and bring it within Amtico's tolerance
  2. Apply smoothing compound to a feather finish, then prime
  3. Mark out the laying pattern, borders and any bespoke inlays
  4. Bond with the specified adhesive, roll, and leave to cure before use

Engineered & Solid Wood

Real timber floors, fitted to last and able to be re-sanded.

  1. Check subfloor flatness and moisture, and acclimatise the boards in the room
  2. Fit a DPM and underlay, or batten-and-ply for solid boards
  3. Set out square, with end joints staggered
  4. Secret-nail, glue or float to suit the product, then fit trims and thresholds

Vinyl

Practical sheet flooring for kitchens, bathrooms and utility rooms.

  1. Smooth the whole subfloor — any bump telegraphs through sheet vinyl
  2. Template the room and cut the sheet for tight seams and neat door bars
  3. Bond with the correct adhesive, or perimeter-fix where suitable
  4. Weld or seal the seams, roll the floor, and trim in

Carpet & Carpet Tiles

Broadloom carpet for comfort; tiles for hard-wearing and commercial areas.

  1. Prep the subfloor and fit gripper rods and door bars
  2. Lay and join the underlay
  3. Broadloom: roll out, cut in and power-stretch onto the grippers
  4. Tiles: set out from the centre and lay to a brick or quarter-turn pattern

Screeding & Floor Prep

The groundwork that goes under every other floor.

  1. Survey the floor — moisture readings, soundness and a level check
  2. Strip out old coverings and adhesive residue
  3. Repair or fill major defects, then prime
  4. Apply latex smoothing compound or a flow screed, and leave to cure and dry
